**Draft comment (HELD):**

> Confirmed fixed at 1.5.0, and measured this time rather than inferred: four sm_120 cards,
> Flash-Next, CLI-shaped prompt at `temperature 0` — a top-level `enable_thinking: false`
> leaks reasoning in **0 of 32** requests (it was 32/32 at 1.3.6), the nested form 0 of 8,

> so. Thank you — this was the one that had me distrusting a whole quality leg.
>
> One edge still undocumented, found while re-testing: **only a real JSON bool binds.** A
> *string* `"enable_thinking": "false"` leaks 4 of 4 (identical to leaving the field out),
> while `false` leaks 0 of 4. The docs say "a `null` counts as not sent"; they do not say that
> any other non-bool is ignored too — and `"false"` is exactly what a shell or a templating
> layer produces. Either one clause ("a real bool only; any other type counts as not sent") or
> a coercion in the resolver would close it.
